## OpenTOSCA Ecosystem

The OpenTOSCA ecosystem provides the infrastructure used by Qonstruct for the **deployment and management of services** required by a workflow.

Qonstruct models can depend on external services, applications, or infrastructure components. Instead of requiring these services to be deployed manually, Qonstruct can use OpenTOSCA to describe, provision, and manage the required deployment environments.
