## Contribute

We welcome contributions to Qonstruct and appreciate your interest in helping us improve the project. Whether you are fixing a bug, improving the documentation, or adding a new feature, your contributions are valuable to the project.

If you would like to contribute, please follow these steps:

1. **Create a branch** for your changes.
2. **Run the linter** to check your changes:

```bash
npx oxlint .
```

3. **Open a pull request** with your changes. Please provide a short description of what you changed and, where helpful, why the change was made.

You can get started by visiting the [Qonstruct repository](https://github.com/LEQO-Framework/low-code-modeler).
