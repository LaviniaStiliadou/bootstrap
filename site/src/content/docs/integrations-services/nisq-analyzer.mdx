## NISQ Analyzer

The NISQ Analyzer is an approach for **automating the selection of quantum computers for quantum algorithms**. It addresses the challenge that different quantum computers provide different hardware characteristics and capabilities, making the choice of a suitable target an important part of quantum software development.

The NISQ Analyzer analyzes the requirements of a quantum algorithm and compares them with the characteristics of available quantum computers. Based on this information, it can identify quantum computers that are suitable for executing a given algorithm.

```text
Quantum Algorithm
       │
       ▼
Algorithm Analysis
       │
       ▼
Required Characteristics
       │
       ▼
NISQ Analyzer
       │
       ├──────────────┐
       ▼              ▼
Quantum Computer A  Quantum Computer B
       │              │
       ▼              ▼
 Hardware           Hardware
 Characteristics    Characteristics
       │              │
       └──────┬───────┘
              ▼
      Suitable Candidates
```

## Role in Qonstruct

Within Qonstruct, the NISQ Analyzer can be used to support the decision of **which quantum computer should execute a quantum circuit**.

After Qonstruct transforms a model into an executable quantum circuit, information about the circuit and the requirements of its execution can be used to analyze available quantum computing resources.

```text
Qonstruct Model
      │
      ▼
Quantum Circuit
      │
      ▼
NISQ Analyzer
      │
      ▼
Quantum Computer Selection
      │
      ▼
Qunicorn
      │
      ▼
Quantum Cloud Provider
      │
      ▼
Quantum Computer
```

This separates the selection of a suitable quantum computer from the actual execution of the circuit. Qunicorn can subsequently provide the middleware layer for communicating with the selected quantum cloud offering.
